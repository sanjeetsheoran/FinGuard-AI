# FinGuard AI - Problem Statement

## 1. Background

The rapid growth of digital financial transactions has increased the exposure of financial institutions to fraudulent activities. Credit cards, online banking, mobile payments, and digital wallets generate a large volume of transactions that need to be monitored continuously.

Traditional fraud detection approaches often rely heavily on predefined rules and manually defined thresholds. Such approaches can struggle when fraud patterns change over time and may generate a high number of false positives.

## 2. Problem

Financial institutions need a system that can identify suspicious transactions accurately while also helping analysts understand why a transaction was considered risky.

Detecting fraud is only one part of the problem. Analysts may also need to investigate customer behaviour, transaction history, device information, merchant activity, and relevant financial policies before making a decision.

The lack of an integrated system for detection, explanation, investigation, and decision support can make the fraud investigation process time-consuming and difficult to scale.

## 3. Existing Challenges

The major challenges addressed by this project are:

- Detection of complex and changing fraud patterns.
- High class imbalance in financial transaction datasets.
- False positives that may affect legitimate customers.
- Limited interpretability of machine learning predictions.
- Time-consuming manual investigation.
- Difficulty accessing relevant financial policies and historical cases.
- Lack of an integrated AI-assisted investigation workflow.

## 4. Proposed Solution

FinGuard AI proposes an AI-powered Financial Intelligence Platform that combines machine learning-based fraud detection with risk scoring, Explainable AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and Agentic AI.

The system will analyse financial transactions, estimate their fraud risk, explain important factors behind predictions, and use specialized AI agents to assist with investigation, knowledge retrieval, compliance analysis, and report generation.

## 5. Expected Outcome

The proposed system aims to provide:

- Reliable fraud detection on previously unseen transaction data.
- Dynamic risk scoring for suspicious transactions.
- Human-understandable explanations for model predictions.
- AI-assisted investigation of suspicious transactions.
- Knowledge-assisted responses using relevant financial documents.
- A centralized dashboard for fraud analysts.
- Automated generation of investigation and analytical reports.

## 6. Project Boundary

FinGuard AI will function as an intelligent decision-support and fraud-intelligence system. It will not independently perform irreversible financial actions such as permanently blocking accounts or transferring funds.

Final high-impact decisions will remain under authorized human or institutional control.
